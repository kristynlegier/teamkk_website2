Executive Summary

I am an undergraduate scholar at Louisiana State University driven by the conviction that the most resilient organizations operate at the dynamic intersection of macroeconomic trade systems, robust enterprise technology, and rigorous data analytics. Through concurrent degree programs in International Trade & Finance and Information Systems & Analytics, I pair analytical quantitative foundations with frontline banking acumen.

In my daily role as an Associate Banker at J.P. Morgan Chase, I put these principles into action.
